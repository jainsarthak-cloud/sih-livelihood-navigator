📌 1. Executive Summary (60-Second Pitch)

"What are we building from scratch?"

We built the SIH Livelihood Navigator, an end-to-end, AI-powered digital ecosystem designed to empower rural and underprivileged citizens by matching them with targeted skill training (NSQF - National Skills Qualifications Framework), government livelihood schemes, local jobs, and self-employment opportunities.

💡 Real-World Problem We Solve

  • Information & Language Barrier: Rural job seekers struggle with complex, formal scheme documents.
  • Skill Mismatch: Youth don't know which NSQF courses fit their education, age, and interest.
  • Lack of Tracking: Government officers lack visibility into candidate progress and employment outcomes.

🌟 Our Solution

  • 🎙️ AI Voice Assistant: Multilingual speech/text interaction for semi-literate users.
  • 🎯 Smart Recommendation Engine: Suitability scoring matching profiles to NSQF courses.
  • 🗺️ Visual Career Roadmap: Step-by-step guidance from training to employment.
  • 📊 Nodal Officer Dashboard: Allows officers to monitor candidates, log interventions, and track outcomes.

🌐 2. Real-World Case Study (Story Explanation)

👨‍🔧 Meet Ramesh (22 years old, 10th Pass, Rural MP)

  1. Voice Entry: Ramesh opens the app and speaks in Hindi: "Mujhe Mobile Repairing seekhna hai".
  2. AI Recommendation: System processes his age (22) and education (10th pass), recommending NSQF Level 4 - Field Technician at a nearby PMKVY center and Mudra loan details.
  3. Visual Roadmap: Ramesh gets a 5-step roadmap: Registration ➔ Training ➔ Certification ➔ Apprenticeship ➔ Job.
  4. Officer Support: A Nodal Officer tracks Ramesh in the dashboard and logs a financial toolkit intervention.
  5. Outcome: Ramesh earns certification and lands a job paying ₹18,000/month.

🧠 5. Recommendation Algorithm Mechanics

Match scores (0-100%) are calculated dynamically using the formula:

Total Score = EducationMatch (30%) + AgeEligibility (20%) + LocationProximity (20%) + InterestAlignment (30%) - BarrierPenalties
  • Education Matching (30%): Verifies minimum education requirement (e.g., 10th pass for Level 4).
  • Age Eligibility (20%): Validates scheme age restrictions (e.g., 18-35 years).
  • Location Proximity (20%): Prioritizes district & state level training centers.
  • Interest Alignment (30%): Keyword vector matching between candidate goal & course content.

⚙️ 8. Software Engineering Methodology

  • Modular 3-Tier Architecture: Decoupled frontend, REST backend, and AI engine for independent scalability.
  • Security First: Salted password encryption (`bcrypt`), JWT token validation, and input sanitization (`express-validator`).
  • Optimized Queries: Mongoose schemas with indexed fields (`district`, `nsqfLevel`).
  • Automated Testing: Integration test suites (`tests/api.test.js`) executed with in-memory MongoDB databases.

🎯 9. NBA Defense Questions & Answers

Q1: How is this different from Naukri or LinkedIn?
Commercial portals target white-collar English speakers with resumes. We target the unorganized rural sector with voice support in local languages, NSQF accreditation alignment, and government officer intervention.
Q2: Why is NSQF integration important?
NSQF standardizes skill qualifications from Level 1-10 across India, ensuring certificates are nationally recognized for jobs and government loans (PMKVY / Mudra).
Q3: What value does the Officer Dashboard provide?
It solves the last-mile monitoring problem. Nodal officers can track drop-outs, provide financial/equipment aid, and measure scheme employment success.
